The Problem

Every mid-sized business is losing leads they never even see.

Businesses running ads, inbound forms, and listings on multiple platforms generate hundreds of leads per month. In most companies, these leads flow into a fragmented mix of inboxes, spreadsheets, and CRMs — where sales teams manually triage, qualify, and follow up.

The result is predictable: response times stretch into hours, unqualified leads consume agent bandwidth, and a majority of cold leads are never re-engaged. The real cost isn't the leads that close slowly — it's the leads that simply vanish into the operational gap.

The Approach

Engineered for reliability, not just function.

Anyone can stitch together a no-code automation that works on a good day. Production systems fail on bad days — so we designed for the bad days first.

Every integration has explicit error handling, every AI call has fallback logic, every external API call has retry policies, and the entire system has end-to-end observability. The result is a workflow that runs reliably in the background without constant babysitting.

On top of that, the scoring logic is transparent and tunable. Business owners can adjust qualification criteria without touching code — a prompt-based control layer makes the system as flexible as the team using it.
